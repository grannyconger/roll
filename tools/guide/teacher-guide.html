Roll with the Market teacher guide

Every phase, what to press, what to say, and what to do when something breaks. Personal Finance, 90 minutes, sections 2B and 4B.

The display and the observer links carry the session, so always open them from the hub. On the display, press F for full screen and D for the dyslexia font.

Before the bell

  • Thinking Logs on desks, one per seat.
  • Hub open. Pick the session for this section, or create one with New 2B session or New 4B session.
  • Display open on the smartboard from the hub link, full screen. It shows the class code.
  • On the hub, Run health check under Assessor. It should read OK. Green before you open the door.
  • Wayground set loaded in a separate tab.
  • Observer link sent to the evaluator.
  • Decide whether to turn on Class code sign-in now. Leave it off unless Google sign-in is likely to be blocked.

Standing moves, all period

  • Circulate. The hub's big button is readable from across the room on purpose.
  • Wait three seconds after every question.
  • Volunteers first, then Pick a student on the hub. It draws from students not yet called on.
  • One chime to reset attention, then wait. Do not talk over the room.
  • Point at the objective at every transition. It is on every screen all period.

After class

Export panel on the hub. Full CSV downloads one row per response with level, override, peer levels, math, archetype, team, and probe answer. Class summary opens a printable page with the percent at Level 3 or higher by game, growth, math accuracy, survey results, and how many students met the goal.

How the hub works

The large button at the top left is always the next thing to do, and it says what it will do. Press it to move the lesson forward. The panels below it are for reference.

Claim, evidence, reasoning

25 to 39
StepsSix screens, each on the display and student screens. Press Next: ... to advance. The button names the next step.
OpenWith the gap, not the definition.

Which was harder, deciding or explaining why?

  1. Claim, evidence, reasoning. The lawyer frame. Claim is the verdict, evidence the exhibits, reasoning the argument that connects them.
  2. Find what is missing. Model one real Game 1 decision aloud, including where you are unsure. Then do it badly and have the class find the gap.
  3. Three parts. Have students point to each part. Then push one strong IN and one strong OUT from Spotlight.
  4. IN averages about $3.50 a round. Ask why anyone would go OUT. Expected: an average is not a promise.
  5. Name the choice you did not make. The move that separates Level 3 from Level 4.
  6. Scoring guide. Students score the weak and strong examples. Expected: 1 and 3.

Both of these are level threes. One went in, one stayed out. The score is the reasoning, not the pick.

Mind traps debrief

68 to 74
DisplayThe seven traps with how many students felt each one.
YouWork from their data, not the list. About two minutes each.
  1. Recency. Return to the streak argument from the Game 1 strategy share. Each roll is independent.
  2. Loss aversion and risk-taking. The two double rounds. Who went IN when behind, and why.
  3. Herding. The probe. Call on a student you noted during the huddle.

You wanted out. Your table said in. What happened in your head when you went along?

ProtectHerding is the moment of the lesson. If you are long, cut recency, not herding.

Closure

81 to 90
YouPoint at the objective. Students answer two questions on screen. Take two aloud.

Can you justify a conclusion with a claim, evidence, and reasoning? Name one thing you did differently in the second game than the first.

CloseOn the tradeoff. The guaranteed dollar was never wrong. It was a different tradeoff from the one most of the room took. With spare minutes, return to herding.

When something goes wrong

Most problems already have a designed path. The rule is the same for all of them: the class never waits for one student.

Sign-in and joining

What you seeWhat to do
Google sign-in fails for several studentsToggles, Class code sign-in on. Students choose "Sign in with the class code instead", then enter the code from the board.
Google sign-in fails for everyone on the live siteThe site address may be missing from Firebase. Firebase console, Authentication, Settings, Authorized domains, add www.coachw.club. Use class code sign-in for now.
"Joining is closed. Tell your teacher."Toggles, Joining locked off.
"Class code sign-in is off. Tell your teacher."Toggles, Class code sign-in on.
"No class has that code"The student typed it wrong, or is in the other section's session. Check the code on the display.
A student arrives lateNothing to do. They join normally, start at the class average balance, and play from the current round. The Flags panel lists them.

During a round

What you seeWhat to do
Timer hit zero but the round is still openThat is by design. Chime, then Lock decisions. Or Add 30 seconds.
A student missed the lockNothing. They are OUT for the round and earn $1. Say nothing unless they ask.
The round will not settleSomeone has not entered a balance. Press Force settle. Balances are rebuilt from decisions and rolls either way.
A student keeps missing the mathAfter two tries the site shows the answer and moves on. Do not stand over them. The Flags panel tracks it.
A balance looks wrongEvery balance is recomputed from decisions and rolls at each settle, so it corrects itself. A student cannot change their own balance.
The dice seem stuck on the displayPress Rolling. Reveal now.
